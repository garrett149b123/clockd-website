You are holding the toy. The shelf tag looks fair. Someone wants this exact gift, and leaving with it today is the easy move. The open question is whether that same toy is cheaper on Amazon, Walmart, or Target.

WorthIt answers that from one photo.

## Why the tag is a weak signal

A holiday display is built to feel urgent. The number on the shelf is what this store wants today. Amazon, Walmart, and Target move on their own, and a typical price can sit under a tag that looks ordinary.

The tag also skips the part you can see with your eyes. Wrong age, a set you already own, a crushed corner on the box. WorthIt checks the price of the item in the photo. You still check that it is the toy you meant to buy.

## What to photograph

Open WorthIt and snap the toy. Include the shelf tag when you can. You need the box and the digits you would pay.

One photo starts a Save check. WorthIt names the item, then shows:

- A deal signal, so you are not left comparing tabs in the aisle.
- A typical price range for that item.
- Online comps from Amazon, Walmart, and Target.

Those comps are estimates, not the live register price at the store you are standing in. Use them to decide, then confirm if you are about to spend real money.

If the tag was cut off in the photo, add the shelf price yourself so the comparison uses the number on the shelf.

## Take it home or put it back

If the shelf price sits under the typical range and under the online comps, buying it here is a reasonable way to leave with the gift. If the tag is higher, put the box back and order the same item.

When the prices are close, the store can still be the better trip. You already have the box. Shipping time, pickup, and tax are yours to weigh. WorthIt does not estimate a delivery date. If you need the gift this week and the tag is only a little above the comps, paying the store can be the simpler call. If the tag sits well above those comps, walk.

## One toy is enough

Photograph the one gift you were already unsure about. Leave the rest of the endcap. A holiday dinner is a different job: type that grocery list if you are choosing a store for the meal. A toy in your hand is a Save photo.

Open Flip on the same scan only if you plan to resell the item. A gift you will give stays on Save.
