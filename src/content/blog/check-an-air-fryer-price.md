## Why an early tag is not a forecast

A pre-Friday sign compares this shelf to the store's older price. It does not compare this model to the same model online. It also does not tell you what Friday will cost. WorthIt does not predict a future sale. It checks the price in front of you.

The useful question is today's tag: the digits on this box, set against a typical range and online comps.

## Buy the fryer or leave it

If the shelf price sits under the typical range and under the online comps, buying it here is a reasonable call. If the tag is higher, put the box back. You can order the same model, or shop again later if you want to wait. Waiting is your call. The scan does not estimate Friday's price.
