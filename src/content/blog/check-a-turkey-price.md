The tag on the turkey case is a small number. It is often a price per pound, sitting under a holiday sign, next to birds that are not the same size. None of that tells you what this bird costs in the cart, or whether the same size bird is less on Amazon, Walmart, or Target.

That is the decision. WorthIt is built for it, before the turkey goes in the cart.

## Why a per-pound tag hides the price

A rate is not the amount you pay. Two birds can share a tag and still be different purchases, because the weight is different. A frozen bag with one printed price is a different offer from a fresh bird sold by the pound. The sign is comparing the case to itself. It is not comparing this package to the same bird online.

WorthIt does not multiply a per-pound rate by the weight. You read the pounds and the price you would actually pay. That total is the number worth checking against a typical range and online comps.

## What to photograph

Open WorthIt and snap the package. Include the shelf tag when you can. You need the bird and the price you would pay. If the weight is printed on the bag, keep it in the frame so the check is for this package, not the one beside it.

One photo starts a Save check. You do not pick Amazon first.

If the tag was cut off in the photo, add the shelf price yourself so the comparison uses a real number. When the sign is only a per-pound rate, add the price you would pay for this bird. A rate alone is not that price.

## What you get back

WorthIt names the item, then shows:

- A deal signal, so you are not left comparing tabs in the aisle.
- A typical price range for that item.
- Online comps from Amazon, Walmart, and Target.

Those comps are estimates, not the live register price at the store you are standing in. Use them to decide, then confirm if you are about to spend real money. A comp for a different weight, or for a frozen bird when yours is fresh, is a different product. Read it that way.

## Buy the bird or leave it

If the price you would pay sits under the typical range and under the online comps, buying it here is a reasonable call. If it is higher, leave the bird.

Confirm the weight, and whether the package is fresh or frozen, before you trust a low tag. WorthIt prices the item in the photo. You still match that result to the bird in your hands.

When the prices are close, the store can still be the better trip. You can take it home today. Shipping, pickup, and tax stay with you. WorthIt does not estimate a delivery date. If you need the turkey this week and the price is only a little above the comps, paying here can be the simpler call. If the price sits well above those comps, walk.
